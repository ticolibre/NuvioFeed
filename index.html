Stremio / Nuvio Addon

TicoLibre Catalogues

A curated addon that adds hand-picked movie and series catalogs straight into Stremio or Nuvio — festival winners, cult studios and the awards that matter. Every title is powered by TMDB in es-MX for region CR.

▶ Add to Stremio ⬇ View manifest.json

What it does

Install once, get organised rows of great films and shows.

🎬

Curated catalogs

Eighteen ready-made catalogs covering acclaimed studios, film festivals and award winners.

🌎

Localized metadata

Titles, posters and details come from TMDB using Spanish (es-MX) for the Costa Rica region.

⚡

Fast & self-contained

Static JSON catalogs and meta files — no accounts, no tracking, no external search.

🧩

Addon protocol

Standard Stremio addon manifest, so it works in Stremio, Nuvio and compatible players.
